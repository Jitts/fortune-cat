Fortune Cat · art direction, round two

Five rungs, from
safe to unhinged.

The riso plates settled the medium. This settles the nerve. Same two-ink press, same cat, five escalating answers to the question of how strange the mark is allowed to be — each one a real, buildable thing rather than a mood board.

01 · ConventionalA lockup. Passes any brand review. The control specimen.
02 · UnconventionalThe cat isn’t drawn. It’s assembled out of statement rows.
03 · WowTwo halftone screens, live moiré, the press still running.
04 · UnexpectedThere is no cat. Then there are two, and you can’t stop seeing them.
05 · FlabbergastedThe mark is a function of your money. Nobody else has your cat.
Read this as a ladder, not a menu. Rung 01 is what you’d ship if the answer were “don’t scare anyone.” Every rung after it trades a little safety for a little memory. Somewhere on this ladder is the highest rung you’d actually defend in a room — that’s the pick.

Keep this drawn no matter which rung you pick — every direction below still needs a boring lockup for the places that refuse to be interesting.

There is no cat path in this file. There is a silhouette function — head circle, two ear triangles, a body trapezoid, a raised paw — sampled every 6.6px, and each scanline is emitted as a statement row: long bar for the payee, short right-aligned bar for the amount.

Up close it is a bank statement. At arm’s length it is unmistakably a cat. That gap is the whole idea, and it is literally true of the product rather than a metaphor about it.

The only direction here whose construction explains what the app does without a word of copy.

The cat is not filled with colour. It is filled with two dot screens eight degrees apart, and the interference between them is genuine moiré — computed by the renderer at whatever size you view it, not baked into a bitmap. Zoom the page and the pattern reorganises, because it is a real optical effect rather than a picture of one.

Then the press keeps running: one plate drifts against the other on a 7.5 second cycle, so the interference breathes. It never resolves and it never repeats visibly.

The one that makes people lean in. Costs you a static export — a PNG of this is just a cat with dots on it, and the whole point evaporates.

Rubin’s vase, run on your two inks. The pouch’s left and right contours are the cats’ faces — forehead, brow, nose, lip, chin, throat — not approximately but to the coordinate. The gold half and the vermilion half are mirrored copies of one path, so the illusion cannot drift out of alignment.

Most people see the pouch first, because the vermilion sits on the centre line where you look. Then the cats arrive and the pouch becomes hard to hold onto. Hover the mark and the cats step back so you can flip it deliberately.

Highest ceiling, narrowest tolerance. It dies at 16px — a gestalt trick needs enough pixels to be tricked by — so this rung ships with 01 underneath it for small sizes.
05

Your Cat

risk ■■■■■ total

05 · Flabbergasted

The mark stops being a picture of a cat and becomes a reading of one. Every line of the drawing is bound to a number the app already has:

geometrybound to
ear angle30-day net trend
body widthbalance vs. your median
whiskersone per active category
fur tickstransactions this month
tailthe 30-day net line, literally
eyesthe existing catState

The tail is not a tail-shaped decoration. It is the sparkline, drawn as a tail. A flat month gives a straight tail; a volatile one gives a kinked one. Drag the knobs and watch the mark re-derive itself — that is the actual generator running, not three saved drawings.

Nobody else has your cat, and yours changes as you spend. That is either the most memorable thing on this page or a support burden about why the logo moved — and both are true.

Where this leaves you

  • Every one of these is production vector. Nothing here is a render or a trace. Whichever rung you point at, the paths in this file are the paths that ship.
  • 02, 03 and 05 are generated, not drawn — they are ~40 lines of geometry each. That makes them cheap to tune and impossible to hand off as a static asset, which is a real trade, not a footnote.
  • Vermilion still never touches the fur. On 01 it is the chop, collar and koban; on 04 it is the pouch, which is the ground the cats are cut out of. The rule held on all five without being argued with.
  • 04 and 05 need 01 underneath them. Neither survives a 16px favicon, so the conventional lockup is infrastructure regardless of which rung wins.
